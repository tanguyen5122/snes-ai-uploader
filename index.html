SNES AI Uploader

A personal publishing tool for the RetroGamingAI and PlantsHistory channels.

SNES AI Uploader is a private tool its owner uses to publish their own short videos (AI gameplay clips from retro games, and short history videos about plants) to their own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram, on a schedule, and to see how those videos perform.

How it works

  • The owner signs in to each platform with that platform's official login and grants permission to post.
  • Finished videos are uploaded to the owner's accounts through each platform's official API at scheduled times.
  • View counts and comments on those posts are read back so the owner can track them in one place.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.
